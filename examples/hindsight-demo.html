Method. Each video is scored as a percentile against the 25 videos published immediately before and after it, which cancels channel growth, seasonality and video age. Differences between buckets are tested by permutation (10,000 resamples, no distributional assumption), and every p-value in the run is corrected for false discovery via Benjamini-Hochberg. Surviving findings are re-tested independently on the older and newer halves of the catalog. Videos younger than 14 days, videos without a symmetric cohort, and buckets under 20 videos are excluded.

These are associations, not proofs. The catalog was not randomised, so a finding is a well-supported hypothesis about a lever — which is exactly why Hindsight ends by designing an experiment instead of declaring victory.
